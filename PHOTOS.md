Photos are illustrative of their stories (file photos of the places and
people, not taken at the 23 November events): the Huang photo is from a
Stanford visit in April 2026, the Benghazi port photo stands for the
sinking of the corvette Merkury there, the Capitol photo stands for the
House majority, the firefighters photo stands for the Cikande factory
fire, the Hormuz photo is NASA's orbital view of the strait, and the
Culiacan bridge photo stands for the Sinaloa capture. One download —
the first Benghazi port photo — hit Wikimedia's rate limit (HTTP 429),
so the second port photo from the same series was used instead.
